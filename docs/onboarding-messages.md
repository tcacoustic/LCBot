# SH Bot: onboarding messages

Short messages to paste into Telegram for teams that won't watch a video (e.g. overseas). Replace `@SHBot` with the bot's real @username before sending.

---

## Everyone: getting started (send first)

> 👋 **Welcome to SH Bot**
> We're moving handovers, special requests and visits into one Telegram bot.
>
> **1.** Open **@SHBot** and tap **Start**.
> **2.** Wait for a ✅ message: Zoe approves everyone by hand.
> **3.** Tap **App** (next to the message box). You'll land on your team's **Home**.
>
> Stuck, or have an idea? Tap the **🔧** at the top right of the App, or send **/feedback** to the bot.

---

## Online team (ON / platform managers)

> 🛒 **SH Bot for the Online team**
> **Home** shows your channels and how many cases are open on each. Tap **Cases** at the bottom to see them all, filter by 🛍 Shopee / 💗 Lazada / TC Webstore and by country.
>
> **You'll get a DM when:**
> • 🆕 a new case comes in on your channels
> • 💬 Livechat or SH add a note to one of your cases
>
> **On a case:** add a note · **Hand back to SH** if you need them to act · **Close (Online)** when you're done.
> **Need SH to contact a customer?** Home → **New Online request**. It goes straight to that store's chat.
> **/report** shows how your channels went this week.

---

## On-Site specialists

> 🔧 **SH Bot for On-Site visits**
> **Home** shows your next visit (who, what, where) and today's list. All times are **your local time**.
>
> **Not free?** App → **My visits** → tap the day → **AM** (10am–2pm), **PM** (2pm–6pm) or **FD** (full day).
>
> **On the day,** send **/visits** to the bot and tap the visit:
> **📍 Arrived** when you get there → **🏁 Leave** when you're done → choose how it went → confirm the charge and payment.
>
> If someone asks to move a visit, you'll get a message with **✅ Accept / ❌ Decline**.

---

## FS (stores)

> 🏬 **SH Bot for stores**
> **Home** shows your next booking, bookings this week and your open requests.
>
> **Book an On-Site visit:** Home → **Book an appointment** → pick the country, a free time and the customer's details. The specialist is told straight away.
> **Look up a case:** search on Home by name, order, product or date.
> **Moved stores?** Send **/store** to the bot.
> **Delivery change for a customer?** See *Special requests* below.

---

## SH / Livechat

> 💬 **SH Bot for SH & Livechat**
> **Handover** (bottom of the App): **Add a case** and **Post handover** are at the top.
> • A new case goes **straight to that platform's chat**, and the PM is told.
> • Add notes as things move; the PM gets each one.
> • Untick a case when it's closed.
> • End of shift: **Post handover** sends TC Online a summary of your shift (nothing new = nothing posted).
>
> **Livechat:** Home → **Clock in / Clock out**, **Week** to pick shifts, **Hours** for your month. The bot asks for your handover when you clock out.

---

## Special requests (for SH, Livechat, ON and FS)

> 📦 **Delivery changes: Special request to Ops**
> App → **Special request to Ops** (on Home):
> order no. → country (🇸🇬 🇲🇾 🇭🇰 🇹🇭) → what's needed → details (no phone or ID numbers) → delivery date (**Another date…** for later ones) → time frame → **🚩 Escalation** if the customer needs it escalated → **Send to Ops**.
>
> • **Before the 2pm cut-off:** goes into the Ops sheet.
> • **After the cut-off (⏰), same-day (🔴) or 🚩:** Ops also see it in their chat straight away.
> • You get a message when Ops pick it up, finish it, or can't do it (with the reason).
>
> 🇭🇰 🇹🇭 HK and TH deliveries go through a partner, so changes there may not always be possible.
