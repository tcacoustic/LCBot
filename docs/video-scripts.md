# SH Bot: how-to video scripts

One phone-screen video per team, with everything that team needs, in about 1.5–2.5 minutes. Ops get a text guide instead (at the end).

Each video has the same shape:
1. Open the App: each team lands on its own **Home**.
2. The team's own screens.
3. Special request to Ops (Livechat, SH, FS and ON only).
4. The 🔧 feedback button, to finish.

## Before you record

- **Use the test bot, with made-up names, phone numbers and order numbers** (e.g. "Alex Tan", "9123 4567", "TCSG-10001"). No real customers or staff in a video that gets passed around.
- **Become each team with `/viewas`.** DM the test bot `/viewas livechat`, `/viewas sh`, `/viewas fs`, `/viewas onsite` or `/viewas on`, then close and reopen the App. Use `/viewas off` when you're done.
- **Set up the data first:** a week of slots, one or two open handover cases, a test specialist with one booked visit, a test platform chat (`/onchat Lazada SG` inside it), a test Ops group (`/opschat` inside it), and your own channels (`/channels @you`) so the PM messages come to you. `TEST_MODE` = `1` on LCBot Test lets you play both sides.
- **Record:** on iPhone, open Control Centre and tap Screen Recording. Turn on Do Not Disturb so no real notifications appear.
- **Read the 🎙 lines** as you go. Keep it slow, and pause a second on each screen.

## 6. Ops (text guide, no video)

Paste into the Ops topic:

> 👋 **Special requests now come through the bot**
> Requests sent **after the 2pm cut-off** (⏰), **same-day** ones (🔴) and **escalations** (🚩) will appear in this topic. Requests before the cut-off go straight into the Special Req sheet as usual. 🚩 = please look at these first.
>
> On each request:
> • **🙋 I'm on it**: so the team knows it's yours. The sender is told.
> • **✅ Done**: when it's sorted. It's ticked in the Special Req sheet too.
> • **🚫 Can't do**: pick a reason (already out for delivery, need more information, or type your own). The sender is told why.
> • **📝 Note to sender**: send them a quick message.
>
> **/requests** lists everything still open. Ticking a row in the Special Req sheet also closes it in Telegram and tells the sender.
> On very busy days, requests may arrive in batches every 30 or 60 minutes. 🔴 and 🚩 ones always come straight away.
> Stuck, or have an idea? Send **/feedback** to the bot.
